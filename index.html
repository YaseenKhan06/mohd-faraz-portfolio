I’m Mohd Faraz, a Video Editor and Motion Graphics Designer based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, India, with over 2 years of professional experience in post-production and visual storytelling.
I specialize in transforming ideas into engaging, high-quality visual content. My work combines precise editing, creative motion graphics, visual effects, and thoughtful storytelling to help brands, creators, and businesses communicate their message effectively.

With expertise in Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Adobe Photoshop, I work across a range of projects — from social media content and promotional videos to explainer videos, brand films, and advanced motion graphics. I believe great visual content is more than just aesthetically pleasing. It should have purpose, communicate clearly, and hold the viewer’s attention.
